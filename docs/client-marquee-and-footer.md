# Client marquee and footer

The original client logo collection now appears in two continuous rows: the first moves from right to left, the second from left to right. Each track contains two identical groups for a seamless loop; duplicate groups are hidden from assistive technology. The pause/resume control pauses both rows, and hovering the logo area pauses movement temporarily. Reduced motion displays the unique logos in static, wrapping rows.

The shared footer follows the supplied reference: dark background, original Suffix logo, partnership copy, contact information, quick links, social icons and six original credential/partner marks. The original logo sits on a white backing to preserve its colors and visibility. Published marks are reproduced without making new certification claims.

Validation includes opposite track movement, pause/resume, reduced motion, six footer images, responsive layouts at 360–1440px, route/image checks and automated accessibility scans. Footer copyright contrast and inline-link underlining were corrected during review. Desktop screenshots are saved in `artifacts/client-marquee.png` and `artifacts/reference-footer.png`.
